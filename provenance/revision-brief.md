# Lulu artwork revision 3

User request: remove the protruding front tooth and make the existing pet's animation more detailed and varied.

Keep the installed custom pet's stable identity, smooth golden-yellow 3D body, large round head, broad orange muzzle, glossy black eyes with white rims, small orange on its head, orange shorts and friendly proportions.

Mouth rule: no teeth in any pose. Closed mouth is a small dark curve. Open mouths show only a dark interior. Eye whites must not be confused with teeth.

Animation beats:
- idle: gentle chest breathing, full eyelid blink, tiny ear/head settle, planted feet.
- movement: alternating contact, down, passing and up phases, counter-swinging arms, slight body lean.
- waving: arm raises, wrist/paw waves with elbow follow-through, arm lowers.
- jumping: crouch, takeoff, airborne peak, descent, soft landing at original baseline.
- failed: surprise, droop, low head and shoulders, sigh, slowly recover.
- waiting: expectant hand gathering/rubbing, head tilt, small palms-up asking gesture.
- running (work): concentrated gaze, hand to chin/temple, small thought nod, no locomotion.
- review: lean to inspect, eyes scan, thoughtful squint, confirming nod, return.
- look: feet and torso remain anchored; head, muzzle and whole eye surfaces lead a clockwise sweep with tiny ear/fruit follow-through.

Pets format remains v2, nine standard states plus sixteen look poses; artwork revision is independent of schema version. No unsupported extra atlas rows.

All artwork edits use built-in image generation. Deterministic scripts only extract, register, compose, clean chroma, encode and validate. Working material and final previews stay local because Library tools are unavailable in this session.
